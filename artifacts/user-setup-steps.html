Your bifrost — zero to talking

one machine · one command · one conversation · one QR · ~20 minutes

STEP 1

One command on your machine

On your Linux box (or WSL on Windows), give this to your AI agent — or type it:

  • curl -fsSL https://veraldar.org/install.sh | bash

It installs everything: bifrost (your bridge app), Yggdrasil (the agent core), local voice, and starts them. It prints what it does — nothing hidden. ~3 minutes.

The onboarding conversation starts by itself.
Your new assistant speaks (through your machine's local voice — free, private):

"Hi. I'm your bifrost. Three quick things and we're done."

1 · Language? — "English" / "Français"
2 · My voice? — "Woman" / "Man" / "calm and deep" — I switch and you hear it immediately
3 · How do I talk? — "Short and fast" / "with more explanations" — change it any time by just asking

Each answer is applied live. Your machine's microphone proves the whole stack works before we continue.
STEP 2

Choose your brain

The assistant presents your options, with prices and data facts:

CODING PLAN (recommended)

cheapest — a subscription (Zai, Anthropic…). The vendor sees your data.

OpenRouter

pay per use — flexible models including AGI-class. Pricier, per-provider data.

LOCAL + FREE

your own machine runs the model. Zero cloud, slower on old hardware.

Paste the key it asks for. Done — the brain is yours, the choice reversible any time.

STEP 3

Your phone becomes the remote

A QR appears (on screen, or at /pair). Scan it with your phone:

  • the app opens in your browser — add to home screen (one tap) — it behaves like a native app
  • a device token pairs your phone — no accounts, no passwords
  • notifications enabled with one permission tap

📱 iPhone: same flow via Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Voice works in-app; iOS quirks are stated limits, not surprises.

STEP 4

Talk.

Hold to talk — or go hands-free. Ask anything. Your words become work on your machine, and the answer comes back as text and speech.

Growing — when you want more

A

Another tree — the TV, the game PC

Install Yggdrasil only on another machine (one command). It joins your private network automatically and appears in the same session list. Your phone: same app, swipe — ask the TV PC to play a movie.

B

Your own safe access point — anywhere

One command on a VPS deploys bifrost-net: your private mesh + WebRTC access point, in the country of your choice. Your data, your server, your laws. The trees connect through it from anywhere.

C

Voice of your own

"I want a calmer voice." "Deeper." "A voice like [someone]." — ask, and the voice changes. Clone your own (with your consent) for a voice that is genuinely yours.

What you never do: write code · configure networks by hand · touch Rust · create accounts · send your voice to a cloud you didn't choose.
