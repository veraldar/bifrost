# Draft — tree door wording (veraldar.org)

DRAFT — publish: website session applies; user approves the pick.
Replaces: "hold to talk". Context: the door on the veraldar.org tree — hold
to open the interaction. Brand voice: terse, terminal-native, verb-first.
No exclamation marks, no cuteness that needs explaining.

## Three options

1. **`hold to start`** — the user's pick; the verb names the outcome, the
   door starts something. Plainest and catchiest.
2. **`hold to cross`** — the lore does the work: bifrost is the bridge;
   holding is crossing it. Map-true without saying "bridge".
3. **`hold to speak`** — the honest literal; names what your thumb actually
   does. Matches product copy ("talk or type from your phone").

## Recommendation

Option 1 (`hold to start`) for the door; "talk" stays in the product copy
where the word list owns it. If the door opens the working miniature (not a
real mic), option 2 is the honest one — you cross into the demo, you don't
speak yet.
